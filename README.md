# The Attitude of Gratitude

A small, Thanksgiving-inspired gratitude reflection app. Five questions help people find something they can be grateful for. Even the smallest seed of gratitude can begin to shift your atmosphere over time. That seed might come from a good moment, what contributed to it, a simple pleasure, another person, or useful support already around you.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. The project uses plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no build step or external dependencies.

## Research grounding

The questions are original prompts inspired by published research; they are not a validated psychological test.

- The GQ-6 is a validated six-item self-report measure of a person's general disposition to experience gratitude. This app does not reproduce or score it. See the [University of Pennsylvania Positive Psychology Center](https://ppc.sas.upenn.edu/node/210) and McCullough, Emmons, and Tsang (2002), [“The Grateful Disposition: A Conceptual and Empirical Topography”](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.82.1.112).
- Watkins, Woodward, Stone, and Kolts (2003) describe gratitude dimensions including a sense of abundance, appreciation of other people, and appreciation of simple pleasures. [Article](https://doi.org/10.2224/sbp.2003.31.5.431).
- Emmons and McCullough (2003) studied “counting blessings” exercises that asked participants to reflect on things they were grateful for. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.84.2.377).
- Seligman, Steen, Park, and Peterson (2005) studied a “three good things” exercise that asked participants to record what went well and why. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.60.5.410).

The app's 0–20 score is a playful reflection aid, not a validated gratitude or mental-health score. A low score just tells the generator to offer simpler starting points.

## Easy ways to customize

- Change the colors in the `:root` variables in `index.html`.
- Edit the questions, answer labels, result titles, and prompt lists in the script.
- Replace the autumn emojis and copy with your own visual style.
